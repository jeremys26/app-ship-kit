## SF Symbols

- Library of 7,000+ symbols; nine weights, three scales; RTL + localization
- Multi-layer drawings for motion
- Downloads: **SF Symbols 8 beta** (macOS Sonoma+), **SF Symbols 7**
- HIG: `sf-symbols`
- Learn more / app links on Resources

---

## Icon Composer

- Layered Liquid Glass icons from one design for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch
- Appearance-mode annotations; Xcode integration; flattened export for marketing
- Requires recent macOS (Resources listed Sequoia+)
- Pair with HIG `app-icons`

## Product bezels

Marketing device frames (Photoshop/PNG unless noted). Examples listed on Resources
include Apple TV, Watch Ultra 3/2, Watch Series 11, Studio Display, iMac, iPad
family, iPhone 17/16, MacBook Air/Pro/Neo, Keynote live-video frames.

Follow Apple Marketing Resources and Identity Guidelines when using bezels.
